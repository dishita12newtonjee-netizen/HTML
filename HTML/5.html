Recipe: Making a Sandwich

  1. Take two slices of bread.
  2. Spread butter on the bread.
  3. Add sliced tomatoes and cucumber.
  4. Add cheese and a pinch of salt.
  5. Place the second slice of bread on top.
  6. Cut the sandwich and serve.
